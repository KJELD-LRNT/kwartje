# Code of conduct

Kwartje follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- **Be respectful and welcoming.** Everyone is welcome, whatever their
  experience, background or identity.
- **Be constructive.** Criticise ideas, not people. Assume good intent, and
  accept that a maintainer may say no to an idea.
- **No harassment.** That means no insults, no personal or political attacks,
  no sexualised language or imagery, and no publishing other people's private
  information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break
these rules, and may block people who keep doing so.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, contact a maintainer privately. Use the
e-mail address on their GitHub profile, or a private
[security report](https://github.com/KJELD-LRNT/kwartje/security/advisories/new)
if nothing else works. Reports are treated confidentially.
